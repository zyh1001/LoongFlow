OUTLINE 3: "Grammar-Guided Genetic Programming over Antisymmetric Expression Trees + Antisymmetry Projection"
==============================================================================================================

CONTEXT
-------
Cold start; only the parent "c0 * z12" (score 0.002839) exists. This outline
explores the widest structural diversity to escape the current near-zero
fidelity basin, but pays for it with higher search cost and higher complexity.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
------------------------
Variables X: expression trees T over the grammar
    T -> c * T | T + T | T * T | z12^k (k odd in {1,3,5})
       | exp(-(a*|z1|^2 + b*|z2|^2)) | exp(-b*|z12|^2) | (1 + d*|z12|^2)
       | conj-correction terms
with terminal symbols {z1,z2,z12} and radial invariants {|z1|^2,|z2|^2,|z12|^2}.
Constraints C:
    C1 every tree must contain an ODD power of z12 as an overall factor
       (projection operator, see below) -> exchange antisymmetry by construction.
    C2 single angular momentum M (all enumerated M=3 in the primary phase).
    C3 tree depth <= 5, node count <= 15 (complexity budget).
    C4 <= 10 fitted parameters.
Objective f(X): evaluator score (fidelity-dominated, complexity-penalized).

SOLVER STRATEGY
---------------
Grammar-guided GP with evaluator-in-the-loop:
  - Population of N=8 candidate expressions (seeded with S0..S3 of Outline 1).
  - Fitness = evaluator score returned by the Evolux evaluator.
  - Selection: tournament (size 3, deterministic RNG seed).
  - Variation: (a) subtree crossover, (b) mutation = insert/remove one envelope
    or correction factor. Mutation is restricted so the ODD-power-of-z12 factor
    is never removed (projection invariant).
  - Elitism: keep the single best expression each generation.
  - Budget: 4 generations (<= 32 evaluator calls), then stop.
  - Multi-start: repeat the whole GP with 3 different RNG seeds; keep global best.

VALIDITY MECHANISM ("antisymmetry projection operator")
-------------------------------------------------------
  For any generated sub-tree N_sym that is SYMMETRIC under z_rel -> -z_rel,
  define the projected expression
      Psi = c0 * z12 * N_sym        (contract the intended odd power down to 1)
  and then verify the odd power is odd:
      PROJECT(T): factor out all powers of z12; if the total exponent is even,
      multiply by one extra z12 (odd-ify). If a factor 1/z12 appears
      (negative exponent), reject the tree.
  This deterministically enforces C1 regardless of what GP produced.
  Additional checks:
    - numeric exchange test (10^4 samples) -> assert ratio ~ -1
    - numeric rotation test with M=3
    - finiteness test on 10^5 points, |z|<=6
    - complexity/depth bounds.
  Guaranteed-valid fallback F =
      "c0 * z12**3 * exp(-0.25*(abs(z1)**2 + abs(z2)**2))".

WHY THIS SOLUTION
-----------------
+ Maximum structural diversity -> best chance to escape a wrong-family basin.
+ Antisymmetry projection guarantees C1 no matter what mutation does.
- Advantage: explores non-obvious forms (conjugate corrections, mixed
  holomorphic/non-holomorphic), matching the task's "mixed structures" family.
- Disadvantage: stochastic and expensive; needs multi-start to control variance;
  riskier to implement correctly; may over-complicate the expression and raise
  the complexity penalty. Least "code-ready" and most luck-dependent -> lowest
  priority unless Outlines 1-2 stagnate.
