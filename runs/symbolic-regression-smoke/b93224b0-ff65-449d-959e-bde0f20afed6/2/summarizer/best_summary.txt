## 3. Strategic Analysis (The "So What?")
- **Root Cause:** Attributable to **both**, but overwhelmingly to *plan quality*. The Plan correctly diagnosed two absent structural features (odd order-3 relative factor enforcing M=3, and the LLL Gaussian), and the Executor added exactly those two features with only three fitted scalars. Execution quality was also high: the evaluator's least-squares converged in 16 evaluations from the physically seeded initialization `a = 0.25`, and the chosen form is analytically transparent (antisymmetry from an odd power, single angular-momentum quantum number, no accidental symmetry breaking). The decisive gain therefore came from the *structural hypothesis*, not from numerical tuning.
- **Key Insight:** Fidelity is carried almost entirely by *symmetry-and-envelope structure*, not by parameter count. A 3-parameter expression with the correct M=3 sector and a negative-definite Gaussian reaches 99.81% fidelity; the parent's failure was categorical (wrong angular-momentum sector, no radial envelope), not quantitative. A second, subtler insight: the fitted exponent lands at `a = 0.2637` rather than the exact LLL `0.25`, i.e. the optimizer used the exponent as a low-dimensional knob to partially absorb Landau-level mixing (λ = 5 is far from the LLL limit) — the residual NMSE 1.894e-3 and this +5.5% exponent offset are almost certainly the same physical effect seen twice.
- **Identified Risk:** The remaining 0.19% NMSE is now the *only* frontier, and the solution offers no stated mechanism to reach it — pushing further by free parameters risks (i) absorbing dataset normalization/global-scale artifacts into `a` and `c0` rather than physics, and (ii) a complexity ratchet in which nested transcendental factors are added for sub-0.1% gains, which the task explicitly discourages. Secondary risk: the complex amplitude `c0 ≈ 4.256·e^{−i162.9°}` is a blind fitted scale; a magnitude of ×4.26 is suspiciously large relative to the natural Laughlin normalization and should be monitored as a possible sign that `a` is over-shrinking the Gaussian and `c0` is compensating (the two are strongly correlated), which would make the extracted exponent physically misleading.

## 4. Actionable Guidance (The "What's Next?")
- **Recommend Stripping:** Before adding anything, run a one-variable ablation on the *current* winner to fix the interpretation: freeze `a = 0.25` (exact LLL value) and refit only `c0`, then also test `a` free with `c0` magnitude pinned. If NMSE barely moves, the 5.5% exponent offset is noise-absorption and should be regularized; if NMSE degrades substantially, the offset is genuine Landau-level-mixing signal and should be *explained* by an explicit correction term instead of hidden in `a`. This is the cheapest, highest-information experiment available and it disambiguates every subsequent step.
- **Recommend Exploration (targeted at the 1.9e-3 residual, one idea at a time):** The child is exactly the "S0" member of the plan's own structure bank; the two physically motivated, untested siblings remain unexplored and directly attack the residual:
  - **S1 — mixed LL correction:** `c0 * z12**3 * exp(-a*(|z1|²+|z2|²) - b*|z12|²)`, initialized at `b ∈ {0.0, 0.25, 0.5}`. With λ = 5 the target is far from the LLL, so an explicit relative-motion Gaussian is the natural place for the missing 0.19%.
  - **S3 — center-of-mass split envelope:** `c0 * z12**3 * exp(-a*|z12|² - c*|z1+z2|²)`, using `|z1|²+|z2|² = ½|z12|² + ½|z1+z2|²`. This tests whether relative and center-of-mass motion want *different* length scales — a strong physical discriminator and a genuinely new structural claim, not a parameter nudge.
- **Recommend Fusion:** Fuse the winning antisymmetric core `z12**3` (now the confirmed M=3 backbone) with a *softened Jastrow zero*: `c0 * z12**3 * exp(-a*(|z1|²+|z2|²)) * (1 + d*|z12|²)`, initialized `d = 0`. This is the plan's S2 and is the only proposed family that can modify the short-distance correlation structure while leaving the long-range envelope and symmetry sector untouched — the correct way to probe whether the residual lives near the pair-coincidence point or at large radius.
- **Warn:** (i) The complexity budget is *not* yet binding (`complexity_penalty = 1.0` at complexity 12), so the next step should spend complexity on a *physically interpretable* correction, never on nested transcendentals. (ii) Because there is no held-out split, any improvement smaller than ~10⁻³ in NMSE can be produced by parameter absorption alone — require every accepted child to come with a *structural* explanation for the gain, and prefer the lower-complexity candidate whenever fidelity differences are within that band. (iii) Treat the fitted `a ≈ 0.2637` and `|c0| ≈ 4.26` as a *correlated pair* in all future lineage tracing; a child that changes one without reporting the other's response should be distrusted. (iv) Do not re-explore even powers of `z12` or any envelope with a non-positive exponent — those families are already disproven (they break C1/C2 or finiteness) and would waste evaluator budget.
