- **Root Cause:** Both, but tilted toward execution. The plan correctly diagnosed the two fatal defects of the parent (wrong Jastrow exponent and missing envelope) and proved antisymmetry/M-sector by construction — high-quality planning. However, the executor did NOT submit the plan's minimal 2-parameter Laughlin form; it autonomously added the non-holomorphic |J|^{2η} factor and anharmonic/phase terms. The plan's structural insight was necessary; the executor's extra non-holomorphic structure was the decisive ingredient for this κ=100 (strong LL-mixing) regime.
- **Key Insight:** The single most important lesson is that the effective Jastrow structure is NOT purely holomorphic. With J³·|J|^{2η} the state behaves as J^{(3+η)}·J̄^{η} with η≈10, i.e., a strongly non-holomorphic, LL-mixed pairing/screening structure. - **Identified Risk:** (i) Complexity ballooned 59→378 with only 6 parameters, largely from re-expressing the Vandermonde three times plus three separate modulus exponents — a maintenance/overfitting trap.
