--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY THIS SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Expected improvement: strong LL mixing (kappa = 100) generically reshapes the
radial envelope away from a pure Gaussian; a quartic invariant term can absorb
that reshaping while keeping the antisymmetric core untouched.
Advantages: retains guaranteed-valid antisymmetry and single-M sector; the
Gaussian is an exact special case (safe fallback); captures a physically distinct
effect (envelope shape) rather than a new Jastrow structure.
Disadvantages: highest complexity of the three outlines; extra parameters risk
boundary hits / over-fitting; may be redundant if the envelope is indeed Gaussian.
