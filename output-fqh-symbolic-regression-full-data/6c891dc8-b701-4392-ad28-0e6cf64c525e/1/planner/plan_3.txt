PLAN 3
=====================================================================
Title: Physics-Seeded Genetic Symbolic Enumeration (PySR-style) with a
       Hard Constraint Post-Filter and Deterministic Fallback
=====================================================================

1. MATHEMATICAL DEFINITION
--------------------------
Variables: expression trees T over the grammar
   terminals {z1, z2, conj(z1), conj(z2), 1, 2, 3},
   unary {exp, conj, square},
   binary {+ , * , -}.
Objective f(T) = fidelity(T)*finite_fraction(T) - 0*complexity(T)
   (complexity is tracked only for the Pareto archive, not the scalar score).
Constraints C:
   C1 node count / parameter budget <= 15 fitted params, integer literals
      |.|<=12, no decimal literals in the return.
   C2 antisymmetry exch(Psi) = -Psi (checked symbolically per individual).
   C3 single M sector (rot-check for some integer M).
   C4 finite on the sampled grid.

2. SOLVER STRATEGY
------------------
- Genetic programming: population 200, tournament selection, subtree
  crossover p=0.7, mutation p=0.3, 40 generations, 5 independent seeds
  (multi-start). Fitness = evaluator accuracy.
- Primitive set chosen so that valid fermionic factors are more likely:
  seed every island with (z1-z2)^3 to bias toward M=3.
- Pareto archive on (complexity, accuracy) to retain diverse structures.

3. VALIDITY MECHANISM (Safety Valve)
------------------------------------
- After each generation, run the sympy antisymmetry + rotation certificate
  on every new individual; REJECT individuals that fail (hard filter).
- Numeric finite gate; REJECT NaN/Inf.
- Deterministic fallback = (z1-z2)^3 if the population dies or stalls.

4. WHY THIS PLAN
----------------
+ Broadest structural coverage (can discover unforeseen motifs).
+ Pareto archive naturally maintains architectural diversity across islands.
- High variance, sample-inefficient, expensive; many candidates wasted.
- Slower to converge to the physically obvious Laughlin-like optimum; the
  discrete search is stochastic (needs many restarts to remove luck).
- Weakest "guarantee" of the three: relies on the search finding structure
  rather than encoding it.
