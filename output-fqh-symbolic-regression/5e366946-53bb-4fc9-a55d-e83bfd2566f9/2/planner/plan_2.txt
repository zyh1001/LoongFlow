PLAN 2 -- Sparse Symmetry-Adapted Dictionary Regression (OMP / Lasso)
=====================================================================

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
------------------------
Variables: a sparse coefficient vector beta over a SYMMETRY-ADAPTED
  dictionary of factors, all pre-multiplied by the mandatory antisymmetric
  seed A(z1,z2) = z12**3 (M=3, odd):
    D = { A*1, A*|z12|^2, A*|z12|^4, A*|Z_cm|^2, A*|Z_cm|^2*|z12|^2,
          A*exp(-|p1|(|z1|^2+|z2|^2)) , ... }
  plus a global complex amplitude c0.
Constraints:
  C1 antisymmetry and C2 M=3 are baked into A, hence hold for every beta.
  C3 finiteness: exponential factors wrapped as exp(-|p| * nonneg).
  C4 sparsity: ||beta||_0 <= 3 (enforced by the selection algorithm).
Objective:
  min_{beta}  ||Psi_hat(beta) - Psi_target||^2 / ||Psi_target||^2
              + lambda * ||beta||_0
  i.e. a sparse model-selection objective on the validation split; the
  L0 term is the complexity-penalty proxy of the evaluator.

SOLVER STRATEGY
---------------
1. Build the dictionary numerically on the training split (features X).
2. Run Orthogonal Matching Pursuit (sklearn.linear_model orthogonal_mp) to
   select k <= 3 atoms greedily; re-solve the linear least-squares for the
   selected coefficients exactly (closed form via scipy.linalg.lstsq).
3. Cross-check with LassoCV (sklearn) over a regularization path.
4. Pick the sparsest model whose validation NMSE is within 1e-3 of the best.

VALIDITY MECHANISM
------------------
Every dictionary atom is pre-symmetrized (A is odd, M=3), so ANY coefficient
vector yields a valid antisymmetric M=3 candidate.  A projection step drops
atoms whose fitted magnitude maps to complexity that increases the evaluator
penalty; a final finite-check runs on |z|=1e4.

WHY THIS SOLUTION
-----------------
Advantages: principled sparsity/penalty control; can discover unexpected
  correction atoms.  Disadvantages: requires a path to generate features
  from data (the executor has no data access -> must delegate fitting to the
  evaluator), higher variance, risk of overfitting the tiny residual, and
  the selected dictionary element may exceed the complexity budget that the
  simpler Plan 1 already respects.  Less robust / less reproducible than
  Plan 1.
