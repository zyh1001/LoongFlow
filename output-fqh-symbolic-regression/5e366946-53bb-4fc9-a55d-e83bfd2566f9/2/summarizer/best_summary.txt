**3. Strategic Analysis (The "So What?")**

- **Root Cause:** Both plan quality and execution quality were high. The *plan* contributed the critical intelligence — it re-derived the score decomposition from the evaluator artifacts, identified that the dominant island_1 loss (0.0055) lived in the complexity penalty while accuracy was near-saturated (0.0008), and prescribed a "strip-to-Schelling-point" structure. The *execution* faithfully realized the prescribed V0 with correct token conventions and analytic guarantees. The outcome is therefore best attributed to a plan-driven strategic insight, cleanly executed.
- **Key Insight:** The physically meaningful content of the target wavefunction for N=2, ν=1/3 is carried almost entirely by the **odd M=3 Laughlin core plus a single-width Gaussian envelope**; the extra `|z12|²` correction term in the 23-op reference bought only a ~2.3× nmse reduction (8.11e-4 → not applicable, but the correction's marginal fidelity contribution is tiny) at a large complexity cost. This confirms the task's own stated principle: when two candidates have similar validation quality, prefer the simpler one. The M=3 sector assignment is also now empirically confirmed (rotation_error ~1e-16), validating the Laughlin ν=1/3 hypothesis over the trivial M=1 parent.
- **Identified Risk:** The child's nmse (1.92e-3) is *higher* than the island_1 record's (8.11e-4). The simplification traded accuracy for penalty. There is a real possibility that the removed `|z12|²`/relative-width degree of freedom encodes genuine Landau-level-mixing (the task explicitly permits non-holomorphic corrections at λ=1/3), so the minimal V0 may sit on the edge of a local optimum rather than at the true Pareto frontier. Additionally, the realized complexity (13) is higher than the plan's optimistic estimate (~9), leaving recoverable penalty headroom.

**4. Actionable Guidance (The "What's Next?")**

- **Recommend Fusion:** This is the highest-leverage next step. Fuse the island_1 record's *single* `|z12|²` correction term into this solution's now-proven low-complexity M=3 core: `c0·z12³·(1 + p3·|z12|²)·exp(−|p1|(|z1|²+|z2|²))`, initializing `p3 = 0` (monotone-safe). This targets the Pareto sweet spot — recovering the ~2.3× nmse gain while adding only 1 parameter and a handful of tokens, aiming to convert the current penalty advantage into a strictly dominating score above both 0.997585 and the island_1 reference.
- **Recommend Stripping:** In parallel, attempt to reduce the *current* candidate's complexity from 13 toward the plan's estimated floor. Re-express the envelope using rotationally meaningful invariants (e.g. rewrite `|z1|²+|z2|²` via `2|Z_cm|²+½|z12|²`, or factor the Gaussian per-electron) and test whether an equivalent token count reduction is available. Keep the analytic guarantee `abs(p1)` inside `exp`. Retain the stripped form only if validation_fidelity does not drop materially — target a strictly Pareto-improving point.
- **Recommend Exploration:** Now that M=3 is empirically confirmed, branch into the *non-holomorphic / Landau-level-mixing* family explicitly endorsed by the task at λ=1/3 — e.g. a conjugate-coordinate or radial correction term multiplied onto the proven antisymmetric core. The evaluator's `rotation_error`/`inferred_angular_momentum` metrics will immediately flag any M-sector violation, so this exploration is low-risk and high-information.
- **Warn:** Do not over-fit by adding a truncated polynomial series `(1 + p3|z12|² + p4|z12|⁴ + …)` beyond one term — accuracy is near-saturated and each extra term inflates the complexity penalty disproportionately. Also guard against the local-optimum trap: the fact that a *simpler* form scored higher is not proof the physics is exhausted, since the removed correction demonstrably reduced nmse. Keep both the minimal V0 and the lightly-corrected V1 alive as separate lineages so the search does not collapse prematurely. Finally, the plan's predicted complexity (~9) missed by ~30%; future planners should base penalty estimates on the evaluator's observed token convention rather than analytic op-counts.
